The Innovation

Why Generic AI Summaries Fail in Workplace Meetings

Traditional LLMs guess owners and invent deadlines to sound helpful. In team deliverables, guessing causes catastrophic project misalignment.

Generic LLM / ChatGPT Summarizer
  • Hallucinates Ownership: When someone says "We need to test the backup", generic AI guesses a speaker arbitrarily.
  • Invents Plausible Deadlines: Fills in "by next week" even when the speaker explicitly said "not sure when".
  • Opaque & Unverifiable: Outputs a flat bulleted paragraph without source sentence links or auditability.
ActionMine AI (The Zero-Guessing Rule)
  • Strict Owner Slot Filling: Collective phrases ("we need to") are marked strictly as Unassigned.
  • Automatic Review Routing: Incomplete items are flagged as Needs review for human sign-off.
  • 1-Click Calendar Sync: Resolves relative dates to ISO calendar events and exports direct to Google Calendar (.ics).

Project Specification

System Architecture & Engineering Methodology

Designed in compliance with the medium-level AI project curriculum: modular, auditable, and production-ready.

1. Dialogue Segmentation

Regex and token-based parser normalizes disfluencies, strips noise, and structures transcript into speaker turns and discrete candidate clauses.

2. Conservative Slot Filling

Distinguishes self-commitments ("I will") from collective desires ("we need to"). If an owner or deadline lacks explicit textual grounding, it defaults to Unassigned.

3. Enterprise Handoff

Converts relative expressions into calendar ISO dates and generates standard RFC 5545 .ics files and Jira/Trello JSON payloads.
